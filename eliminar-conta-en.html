Delete your account and data — PagaCerto

PagaCerto app, by Luis Filipe Baptista Rebelo Afonso (brand “L. Apps Studio”).

The account is optional and is only used to sync between devices. You can delete it at any time.

How to delete it in the app (recommended)

  1. Open PagaCerto and sign in to your account (if you are not already signed in).
  2. Go to Settings → Account and sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and cloud data and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app

Send an email to dontasko.geral@gmail.com with the subject “Delete PagaCerto account”, from the email address you used to create the account. We will reply and delete the account as soon as possible.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Email and internal account identifierDeleted.
Payments synced to the cloud (already encrypted on your device)Deleted.
Passphrase verification data (salt and verifier)Deleted.

What is not deleted by this request

  • Data stored on your phone (payments and settings). It stays on the device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob for advertising, which is managed by Google. You can manage or limit advertising in your Google Account settings.
  • Technical backup copies at the hosting provider may exist temporarily; they are not accessible to PagaCerto.

Deleting only some data

You can delete payments one by one in the app. If sync is on, the deletion is passed on to the cloud. To restart the cloud with a new passphrase, use Settings → Account and sync → I lost my passphrase, which deletes the encrypted cloud data but keeps the account.
