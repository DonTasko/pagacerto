Privacy Policy

PagaCerto · Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Luis Filipe Baptista Rebelo Afonso (brand “L. Apps Studio”), Avenida de Espanha, Lote 7, Rés-do-chão, 6000-078 Castelo Branco, Portugal, tax ID (NIF) 201701588. Contact for privacy questions: dontasko.geral@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • Without an account, your payments stay only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • With an account (optional), your payments are encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, before being sent to the cloud. We cannot read them.
  • The free Android app shows ads (Google AdMob), only after your consent. Your payments are never used to choose ads. We do not sell data.
  • We never ask for access to your email, contacts, location or bank account.

4. Legal basis

Performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for the account and sync; legitimate interest (point (f)) for security and abuse prevention. We do not intentionally process special categories of data; please avoid writing health or similar data in the notes.

5. Processors and transfers

We use Supabase (hosting and authentication), with the database in the London region (United Kingdom). The United Kingdom benefits from a European Commission adequacy decision, and the transfer relies on it. Apart from Google (ads, see 5-A), we do not share data with third parties for marketing.

If you use “Add to Google Calendar”, a link opens on Google with the title and date you chose; from then on Google’s policy applies. Confirmation and account-recovery emails are sent through the authentication platform (Supabase) or a transactional email service, if one is configured later.

5-A. Ads (free version of the Android app)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show a banner and, occasionally, a full-screen ad. For that, Google may collect and process on your device data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device data and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google acts as controller of that data, under Google’s policy.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we first show Google’s consent form (UMP). Without consent, personalised ads are not requested. You can change your choice in Settings → Ads → “Ad and privacy preferences”, and reset the advertising ID in Android settings. Your payment data is not sent to Google.

A future paid version may remove ads.

6. How long we keep data

For as long as the account exists. If you delete the account (Settings → Account and sync → Delete account and cloud data), we immediately remove the account, the encrypted payments and the key from the cloud. Provider backups may persist for a limited period set by the provider. Data on the device is only erased through the app (“Delete all data”) or by uninstalling it.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app (edit/delete payments, delete the account). For the rest, write to dontasko.geral@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or with the authority in your own country.

Important: since we do not have your passphrase, we cannot recover the cloud data if you lose it. You can start over with a new passphrase, which deletes the encrypted data in the cloud.

8. Security

HTTPS connection; AES-256-GCM encryption on the device with a key derived through PBKDF2-SHA256; database access restricted per user (Row Level Security). No system is infallible; if a breach occurs that puts you at risk, we will notify the CNPD and those affected as required by law.

9. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a significant way, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
